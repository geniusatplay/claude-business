# Getting product images we're allowed to use (affiliate video businesses)

Rule: never build AI images from Amazon listing photos or from other sites' photos without written permission. Use one of these, in this order:

1. **Free samples:** TikTok Shop free-sample requests (after joining TikTok Shop Affiliate) and Amazon Creator Connections campaigns. Film and photograph the real item.
2. **Brand permission email** (below). Save every written "yes" in the business folder under `Permissions/<brand> <date>.md` with the sender, date, products and exact wording.
3. **Brand affiliate kits** from the brand's own affiliate program (Impact, ShareASale, etc.), used within that program's license.
4. **Buy and shoot it** (within the $50 cap).

Reverse image search (Google Lens, AliExpress, Alibaba) is for research only: find the brand, the official store, colour names, and spot knockoffs. Never use those photos, and never promote a lookalike as the Amazon item.

## Finding the right contact
- Amazon listing → "Visit the <Brand> Store" → brand website → Contact, Press, Partnerships or Affiliates page.
- Prefer a partnerships/marketing email over a general support form. Check the brand's Instagram bio too.
- One email per brand. Never bulk-send, never buy lists. Follow up once after 7 days, then stop.

## Email template (Randy sends it from his own email)

Subject: Permission to feature <Product> in short styling videos

Hi <Brand> team,

I'm Randy, and I make short styling videos for <channel name> on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram (<handle>). I'd like to feature your <product name> (<Amazon ASIN or link>) in a few videos that link to your listing.

Could I have your permission to use your official product photos as the reference for AI-assisted styling scenes? Every video is labeled as AI-assisted and as an affiliate post, and the product is always shown exactly as you sell it, with real footage of the item. If you'd prefer, I'm also happy to film a sample instead.

If yes, a one-line reply confirming which images I can use is all I need. I'll send you links to anything that goes live.

Thanks,
Randy
<channel name> · <handle> · <email>

## Agent steps
1. Pick 5–10 products that fit the niche and pass the Compliance & Disclosure Checker.
2. Find each brand's contact; fill in the template; put the drafts in `Outreach/<date>.md` for Randy to send.
3. Log replies and permissions; only products with permission (or our own footage) go to the First-Frame Prompt Artist.
