# Short-Video Affiliate Marketing Playbook (YouTube Shorts · TikTok · Facebook/Instagram Reels)

Prepared: 2026-10-21 · Research Lab · For: one operating agent per affiliate venture (V1 Fashion, V2 Home/Gifts). Read it together with `affiliate-ventures.md`.

Legend: **[V]** = verified on an official platform page or official statement · **[S]** = secondary or trade source · **[U]** = practitioner heuristic or inference. Test it.

**Hard limits on every tactic here:**
- No bots, automation, bought followers, views or likes, engagement pods, comment spam, or unsolicited link DMs.
- These break the TikTok Community Guidelines **[V]**, YouTube spam policies, Meta's fake-engagement enforcement **[S]**, FTC 16 CFR 465 on fake social-media indicators **[S]**, and Amazon's rule on solicited messages only **[V]**.

## 1. How each recommender ranks short video now

### 1.1 TikTok For You feed
- **Official [V]** ([TikTok Newsroom, "How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou"](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/), 2020, re-stated 2023). Signals are:
  - **user interactions:** likes, shares, comments, follows, the user's own creations, and watching to completion;
  - **video information:** captions, sounds, hashtags;
  - device and account settings, which carry lower weight.
- "Whether a user finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end" is a strong signal. **Follower count and past hits are not direct factors.** That is good news for new accounts.
- **Eligibility gate [V]** ([TikTok CG: Integrity & Authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)):
  - "unoriginal or reused material without anything new" is **ineligible for the For You feed**;
  - unlabeled realistic AI may be restricted.
- **Practical model [S/U]:** the video is served to a small test batch. Completion, rewatch, shares, saves, comments and profile visits per view decide whether it moves to the next batch. Shares and saves are commonly treated as the strongest intent signals **[U]**.
- **Search is a second distribution engine.** Captions, on-screen text and spoken words are indexed **[S]** ([Virlo 2026](https://virlo.ai/blog/tiktok-seo-how-do-i-rank-videos-in-tiktok-search-2026), [tlinky](https://tlinky.com/tiktok-search/)).

### 1.2 YouTube Shorts
- **Key Studio metric [V-ish]:** "Viewed vs. swiped away", defined in YouTube Studio as "the percentage of times that viewers viewed your Shorts versus swiped away" ([vidIQ 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/) quoting Studio).
- **How distribution expands [S]:** Shorts are tested on a seed audience and expanded if viewers watch through, replay and engage.
- **Benchmarks are unofficial [S]:**
  - a 70–90% viewed rate is "good";
  - below 50% means the hook is failing.
- Average percentage viewed above 100% is possible through loops and rewatches **[U]**.
- **Policy gate [V]** ([YPP policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)): templated or mass-produced AI content loses monetization, and that includes Shopping, which depends on YPP.

### 1.3 Instagram Reels / Facebook Reels
- **Instagram [S, Mosseri Jan 2025]** ([Kompozy summary](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance), [BlckAlpaca 2026](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/instagram-algorithm-2026)). The top three signals are:
  - **watch time**;
  - **likes per reach**, which matter slightly more with followers;
  - **sends per reach** (DM shares), which matter slightly more with non-followers.
- **Originality [S]:** original content is favored and detected reposts are replaced by the original.
- **Facebook [S]** ([TechCrunch 2025-07-14](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/14/following-youtube-meta-announces-crackdown-on-unoriginal-facebook-content)):
  - repeat reposters lose distribution and monetization;
  - duplicate videos are demoted.
- **Trial Reels [S]** (launched Dec 2024, [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2024/12/10/instagrams-new-trial-reels-feature-lets-creators-test-content-with-non-followers)):
  - shown to non-followers first;
  - insights arrive after 24 h;
  - can auto-share to followers after 72 h if the reel performs.
  - Use them as the native A/B tool.

### 1.4 Signal priority for 15–20 s affiliate clips [U]

| Rank | Signal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook hold (viewed vs. swiped / 2-s retention) | Gates all further distribution |
| 2 | Completion % and rewatch (loop) | A strong weight on TikTok [V]; watch time on IG [S] |
| 3 | Shares / sends per reach | Strongest reach signal for non-followers on IG [S]; high intent on TikTok [U] |
| 4 | Saves | Signals "buy later". Very relevant to shopping |
| 5 | Comments (substantive) | Topic signal plus social proof |
| 6 | Early velocity (first 1–3 h relative to the account's baseline) | Decides whether the test batch expands [U] |
| 7 | Product-link CTR and orders | TikTok Shop and YouTube Shopping use commerce signals for shoppable distribution [U] |

## 2. Creative rules for 15–20 s affiliate clips

### 2.1 Hook (0–2 s)

You have one shot at it. Open on the **payoff or the tension**, never a logo or a slow pan.

- **Visual hook:** the frame-0 image is already "in motion". The AI first frame must be the most striking frame, e.g. a finished outfit or a glowing room. MiniMax animation starts **from** the striking frame.
- **Text hook:** on-screen, 3–7 words, readable in under 1 s, placed in the safe zone (avoid the bottom ~20% and right ~15% UI areas) **[U]**.
- **Spoken hook** (Randy's voice) at the same time as the text, using the search keyword. Templates:
  - **Contrarian:** "Stop buying graphic tees after 30."
  - **Specific number:** "The $24 lamp that fixed my ugly corner."
  - **Problem then promise:** "Sweating through shirts in Florida? Try this fabric."
  - **Identity:** "If you're 5'9" and your chinos bunch, watch this."
  - **Gift urgency (Q4):** "Gift for the guy who has everything, under $30."
- **Never:** fake urgency ("only 2 left!" when unverified), fear or disgust tactics (TikTok Shop AIGC ban **[V UK]**), or on-screen prices (Amazon policy **[V]**).

### 2.2 Structure and pacing for 15–20 s

| Time | Beat | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 s | Hook (visual + text + voice) | Payoff first |
| 2–6 s | Proof / reveal #1 | Cut every 1.5–2.5 s **[U]**. MiniMax clips are 5-s units, so trim them |
| 6–13 s | Value: 2 more looks or uses, one honest "who it's for / not for" | Real b-roll ≥3 s: hands, fabric, switch (TikTok Shop requires ≥3 s dynamic content **[S]**) |
| 13–17 s | CTA + disclosure | "It's in my showcase, I earn a commission if you buy" + on-screen "#ad · AI visuals" |
| 17–20 s | Loop bridge | The last frame matches the first frame, to drive rewatches **[U]** |

- **Length:** 15–20 s is right for product clips. A 7–12 s variant is worth testing for pure-visual products, because completion rates are higher **[U]**.
- **TikTok Shop under 8 s:** videos shorter than 8 s don't count toward the pilot early-unlock activity requirement **[V]**.
- **Audio:**
  - Randy's voice beats AI voice. AI voice is banned in TikTok Shop LIVEs, and its status in recorded videos is disputed **[S]**.
  - Use **commercially licensed** music: TikTok's Commercial Music Library for Business, YouTube Audio Library, Meta Sound Collection.
  - Trending copyrighted songs can be restricted on commercial or affiliate content **[U]**.
- **Captions:** burn in your own large captions (most viewers watch muted **[V FTC note]**). Each caption line is ≤6 words, with the keyword highlighted.

### 2.3 Variety rule (anti-"inauthentic")

Rotate at least 4 formats per week per account, so no 7 consecutive videos share a template **[U, from YouTube's "minimal variation" language [V]]**:

1. **Fashion:**
   - "1 piece, 3 outfits"
   - "Fit check: size guide"
   - "Florida heat test (real fabric close-up)"
   - "Don't buy X, buy Y"
   - Comment-reply video
2. **Home:**
   - "Corner glow-up"
   - "3 gifts under $30 for ___"
   - "Desk setup before / after"
   - "Is it worth it? (honest con)"
   - Comment-reply video

## 3. Search optimization vs. hashtags

| Platform | What gets indexed | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Caption, on-screen text, spoken words (auto-transcribed), hashtags, sound **[V partial: captions/sounds/hashtags; S: speech/OCR]** | Say the exact keyword within the first ~3 s **[S]**. Put it in the first on-screen text and in the first 50–80 characters of the caption **[S]**. Use 3–5 specific hashtags (#mensfashion #linenshirt #giftsforhim). Use TikTok search autocomplete as keyword research. |
| YouTube Shorts | Title, description, spoken words (captions), hashtags (#Shorts is optional) **[U]** | Title ≤60 characters: "[Keyword] + payoff", e.g. "Men's Linen Shirt for Florida Heat (3 Ways)". Description: 1–2 keyword sentences, then the disclosure, then 3 hashtags. Upload accurate captions. |
| Instagram | Caption keywords, on-screen text, alt text, max **5 hashtags** (cap reported Dec 2025 **[S]**, [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)) | Keyword-rich first line. Mosseri has repeatedly said hashtags matter little for reach **[S]**. Use 3–5 relevant ones for categorization. |
| Facebook | Caption text, Page category, engagement | Keyword in the first line. Hashtags are minor **[U]**. |

**Topic training (content-based targeting):**
- On a new account the algorithm learns "who to show this to" from the **content itself** and from who engages with it.
- To train it:
  1. Keep one niche per account.
  2. Use the same 5–10 core keywords repeatedly (spoken, written and captioned).
  3. Make the first 10–15 posts tightly on-niche. **No off-topic viral attempts.**
  4. Use consistent visual identity (color grade, caption style) and the same narrator.
  5. Write a profile bio stating the niche in keywords ("Menswear basics for guys 30+ · Florida-proof outfits").
- Before posting, spend 10 minutes engaging genuinely in-niche from the account (watch, like or comment on niche videos), so the account's own interest graph matches **[U]**.

## 4. Titles, descriptions and captions: templates

**TikTok caption (≤150 characters for the visible part):**
`#ad (commission) Men's linen shirt that survives Florida humidity: 3 ways to wear it. Fit runs big, size down. AI-styled visuals. #mensfashion #linenshirt #floridastyle`

**YouTube Shorts title / description:**
- Title: `Best Men's Linen Shirt for Hot Weather (3 Outfits)`
- Description: `I earn a commission if you buy through the tagged product. Visuals partly AI-generated; product shown is the real item (color/fit matched). Fit note: runs large. #mensfashion #linenshirt #summeroutfits`

**Instagram / Facebook caption:**
- First line has the keyword and the payoff.
- Second line: `Ad · I earn from qualifying purchases · AI-generated model, real product`.
- Then up to 5 hashtags.
- Use Meta's AI info toggle and Paid Partnership / Branded Content where applicable.

**Bio:**
- Niche line.
- "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" (once approved) **[V]**.
- Link-in-bio, or TikTok Showcase.

|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Bio link needs a Business account with 1k followers, or other conditions **[U: check in-app]**. Otherwise no links. Build an audience first. | **TikTok Shop showcase plus product anchor in the video** (1k followers, pilot max 3 shoppable/day) **[V]**. Amazon links only via a bio link page. Never Amazon links in comments or DMs unsolicited. | Spoken + on-screen + caption "#ad". AI toggle on. Use the commercial content toggle where required. |
| YouTube Shorts | Description and comment URLs are **not clickable [V]**. Use channel profile links and a "related video" pointing to a long-form video whose description holds the links. | **Shopping product tags** (Amazon since 2026-08-27 **[V]**), shown as an in-Short product sticker. | Spoken + on-screen. Enable "Includes paid promotion" **[U: YouTube's checkbox; affiliate-only use is debated]**. Altered/synthetic toggle **[V]**. |
| Instagram | Bio link | Affiliate product tags in Reels (about 1k followers, 18+) **[S]** | Caption first line + on-screen + spoken. AI info label. |
| Facebook | Bio / Page link | **Affiliate Partnerships: Amazon product tags in Reels** **[V]** | Same as Instagram. Branded Content tool is optional **[V Amazon help]** but not sufficient on its own **[V FTC]**. |

**FTC placement rules [V]:**
- The disclosure goes **in the video**, both said aloud and shown on screen.
- In captions it goes **before** "more" and is not buried among hashtags.
- Plain words only: "Ad", "I earn a commission".

## 6. Posting frequency and times

| Platform | Frequency (per venture account) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 1–2 per day; shoppable ≤3 per day while in the pilot **[V cap]** | Consistency over volume [U] |
| YouTube Shorts | 1 per day | Same core clip with a re-cut hook and title (not a duplicate file) [U] |
| Instagram Reels | 4–7 per week; use Trial Reels for hook tests **[S]** | |
| Facebook Reels | 4–7 per week | Native upload only, no TikTok watermark (unoriginal or duplicate demotion **[S]**) |

- **Times [U]:** start with 11:00–13:00 and 18:00–21:00 local time in your audience's timezone (ET for Florida), weekdays plus Sunday evening. After about 20 posts, use each platform's "followers active" analytics and shift to the top 2 windows.
- **Staggering:** don't post the identical upload to all four platforms in the same minute. Stagger by 2–6 h and vary the hook text **[U]**.
- **Re-cuts:** re-export per platform with no cross-platform watermarks.

## 7. Niche account strategy

- **One account per venture per platform:** 8 accounts in total. Don't mix fashion and home on one account.
- **Handle:** keyword plus personality, e.g. `@fitsfor30` or `@glowupcorner` **[U: check availability]**.
- **Content pillars per account:**
  - Fashion: Styling · Fit/sizing · Florida/occasion · Honest comparisons.
  - Home: Glow-ups · Gift lists · Worth-it tests · Seasonal.
- **Ratio:** about 70% value or entertainment, 30% direct shoppable **[U]**. TikTok Shop pilot caps shoppable posts anyway.
- **Trust assets:**
  - Pin 3 videos: "Who I am / how I pick products / why I use AI visuals (and what's real)".
  - Being transparent about AI turns the risk into a brand trait.

## 8. A/B testing hooks and thumbnails

- **Instagram:** use Trial Reels. Post 2 hook variants as trials and promote the winner to followers after 24–72 h **[S]**.
- **YouTube:**
  - Shorts thumbnails are selected from frames in the mobile upload flow **[U]**. Make the frame-0 AI image double as the thumbnail.
  - The "Test & Compare" thumbnail A/B tool applies to long-form **[U: Shorts availability unverified]**.
  - For Shorts, test with sequential re-cuts instead: same body, different first 2 s, posted 48+ h apart.
- **TikTok:**
  - No native A/B for organic. Use sequential re-cuts with different hook text, voice line and first frame.
  - Wait at least 48 h between variants. Change one variable at a time.
  - Delete nothing (deletions can look spammy **[U]**). Just stop iterating losers.
- **Test log columns:**
  - video ID, variable changed, hook text, first-frame type
  - 2-s hold (or viewed vs. swiped)
  - completion %, shares per 1k views, saves per 1k views
  - product clicks, orders
- **Minimum sample:** judge only after a variant reaches about 1,000 views or 48 h, whichever comes later **[U]**.

## 9. Comment and community tactics (allowed)

- **Reply to every substantive comment within the first 1–2 h.** Early conversation helps velocity **[U]**.
- **Video replies to comments:** TikTok "reply with video" and YouTube "Create Short from comment". Questions like "what size are you?" or "does it fit tall guys?" are free, high-relevance content.
- **Pin a comment** with a fit note, the "AI visuals, real product" note and "tap the product tag". On YouTube Shorts the pin is for info only, because links aren't clickable **[V]**.
- **Ask a real question in the caption,** e.g. "Linen or performance poly for 95°?", to drive genuine replies. Do not use "comment 1 if…" engagement bait (TikTok makes pressure-to-engage content FYF-ineligible **[V]**).
- **Handle "this is AI" comments honestly:** "Yep, the model is AI. The shirt is real, here's my hand-held close-up." Never deny it.
- **DM links only when the user asks** ("send me the link") **[V Amazon solicited rule]**. Platform auto-DM tools that fire on a keyword comment are a grey area. Use them only when the user explicitly requests it, and with platform-native tools **[U]**.
- **Collaboration:** reply-stitch or duet larger creators in-niche with added value. Don't comment-spam other creators' videos with self-promotion.

## 10. Analytics and kill / iterate / scale rules

**Track per video (at 24 h and 7 d):**
- reach / views
- 2-s hold (TikTok "retention at 2 s"; YouTube "viewed vs. swiped away"; IG "skip rate" where available **[U]**)
- average % watched / completion, rewatch rate
- shares, saves and comments per 1k views
- follows per 1k views
- product-tag CTR and orders / GMV, commission, return rate

**Decision rules [U: starting thresholds; recalibrate to your own medians after 30 posts]:**

| Condition (at 48 h) | Action |
|---|---|
| Viewed-vs-swiped <50% or 2-s hold <60% | **Hook problem.** Keep the body and re-cut a new first 2 s (new image, text and line) |
| Hook OK, completion <30% (for 15–20 s) | **Body or pacing problem.** Tighten to 10–12 s and add a cut every 1.5 s |
| Completion ≥50%, shares+saves <5 per 1k | Content entertains but doesn't convert interest. Add a "who it's for" line or a more useful angle |
| Views strong (≥3× account median), product CTR <0.5% | **Offer problem.** Wrong product or price perception. Swap the product, keep the format |
| CTR OK, orders 0 after ~500 clicks | Landing problem (listing reviews, stock, price). Drop the product |
| ≥3× median views **and** ≥1 order per 1k views | **SCALE:** make 3–5 new variants (new hooks, settings, recipients) of the same product + format within 72 h, and cross-post re-cuts |
| A format fails 5 times in a row (below median on hold and completion) | **KILL** the format for 30 days |
| Account median views flat or declining for 14 days | Review niche focus. Make the next 10 posts strictly core-keyword. Check for policy strikes or labeling issues |

**Weekly review (agent output):**
- top 3 and bottom 3 videos with the cause of each;
- the winning hook pattern;
- next week's 10 planned videos;
- a compliance audit sample: 3 random videos checked for disclosure, AI label and product accuracy.

## 11. 30-day launch plan (brand-new accounts, per venture)

**Week 0 (Days 1–2): Setup**
1. Create TikTok, YouTube, Instagram Professional and Facebook Page / Professional Mode accounts. Use the same handle, keyword bio and a profile image that is not an AI person.
2. Buy or receive 1–2 real products. Photograph them on a neutral background, in daylight, from 6 angles. Shoot 30 s of hand b-roll per product.
3. Build the asset kit: caption style, disclosure end-card ("#ad · I earn a commission · AI-generated visuals, real product"), and the loop bridge template.
4. Keyword list: 10 core terms from TikTok search autocomplete and YouTube search suggestions.
5. Make 10 minutes a day of genuine niche viewing and engagement on each new account.

**Week 1 (Days 3–9): Topic training, no selling**
- 1 per day on TikTok and Shorts, 4 on IG/FB.
- Pure value: styling tips, room ideas, "how to pick X".
- Products can appear, but there are no links yet. Add an affiliate disclosure if there is any expected commission relationship **[V FTC]**. Otherwise just be honest.
- Post the 3 pinned trust videos.

**Week 2 (Days 10–16): Format testing**
- Test 4 formats × 2 hooks each.
- On IG, use Trial Reels for the hook pairs.
- Log the metrics and apply the decision table.
- Start answering comments with video replies.

**Week 3 (Days 17–23): Double down**
- Make 60% of output the top 2 formats.
- **When TikTok reaches 1,000 followers**, apply to TikTok Shop Affiliate. Then pass age and ID verification and the probation quiz.
- Start shoppable videos, at most 3 per day, only products with a Shop Performance Score of 95% or higher **[V]**.
- Request free samples from sellers once eligible.

**Week 4 (Days 24–30): Monetize and Q4 push**
- **V2:** launch the "Gifts under $30" series (daily through Dec 20).
- **V1:** "Gifts for men" crossover plus the "Holiday party outfit" series.
- **Apply to Amazon Associates once at least 1 platform reliably drives 3+ potential buyers a week.** Target mid-November for the Black Friday / Cyber Monday wave. Then connect Facebook Affiliate Partnerships (Amazon).
- Day 30 review:
  - account medians;
  - winning formats;
  - set next month's thresholds;
  - check YPP progress (500 subs and 3M Shorts views in 90 days **[S]**) toward YouTube Shopping.

**Day-30 targets (typical new niche account) [U]:**
- 30–45 TikTok and Shorts posts;
- 300–2,000 followers on the lead platform;
- 1–3 breakout videos (≥10k views);
- first TikTok Shop orders if 1k followers were reached.
- **Missing these is normal. Most accounts take 60–120 days.**

## 12. Pre-publish compliance checklist (agent must pass every item)

- [ ] Product in the video matches the real listing (color, pattern, size, pieces, features). No AI "upgrades".
- [ ] Source image is Randy's own photo or a licensed supplier asset, **not an Amazon listing image**.
- [ ] ≥3 s of real or dynamic product footage, with no full-still video.
- [ ] Randy's real voice. Opinions are his, and none come from an AI person.
- [ ] Platform AI toggle on, plus on-screen "AI-generated visuals".
- [ ] Spoken and on-screen commission disclosure, plus "#ad" at the start of the caption.
- [ ] No prices on screen, no unverifiable claims (health, durability, "best"), no fear or disgust hooks.
- [ ] No real-person, celebrity or creator lookalikes.
- [ ] Licensed music, native upload, no other platform's watermark.
- [ ] Format differs from the last 2 posts. No repeated template.
