# Browser + AI-Agent Business Models: Ranked Shortlist (Oct 2026)

**Brief:** Legal in the US, nothing deceptive, $0-$30 to start (ideally $0-$10), run almost entirely from a browser with AI agents doing the production work, revenue visible in a dashboard, a believable path to $100/day, and a documented ceiling of thousands to tens of thousands per day for the best operators.

**Operator:** One US adult, age 35. They handle ID/KYC, tax interviews (W-9), payout setup, any live client calls, and final quality checks.

---

## 0. Ground truths before you read the rankings

1. **Most people make close to nothing.** That holds on every platform where data exists.
   - Gumroad's 2020 data: median creator made **$70 for the year**. About 17% made $1,000 or more. The top 1% took about 60% of revenue. ([Silicon Florist summary](https://siliconflorist.com/2021/01/12/what-happened-in-the-gumroad-creator-community-in-2020/))
   - TrustMRR's Stripe-verified dataset: **67.8% of listed startups have under $1,000 lifetime revenue**, and 0.9% have passed $1M. ([solopreneurpage roundup](https://solopreneurpage.com/blog/micro-saas-ideas-successful-examples-solo-founders))
   - About 65% of Etsy sellers make under $100 a year, and the median is about $574/month in gross revenue. Both figures come from third-party analyses. ([Printify](https://printify.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/), [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/digital-products-etsy-profitability))
   - Written Word Media's 2025 survey: about 44% of indie authors earn $100/month or less. That sample leans toward serious authors. ([summary](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/how-much-do-kdp-authors-earn/), [WWM](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-author-survey/))
2. **The "top performer" examples below are survivorship-biased.** People publish revenue screenshots after they win. Many of the figures are self-reported, and some are years old. I mark which numbers are verified and which are self-reported.
3. **AI makes production close to free for everyone.** Supply in every template, print-on-demand and content marketplace is going up while demand stays flat. The defensible edge is now **distribution, niche specificity and real usefulness**, not output volume. Plan to win on quality and focus.
4. **$100/day is about $3,000/month.** For a new solo operator that is a good result, not a baseline. Realistically it takes 3-12 months, usually from a mix of several streams.
5. **The $30 cap is tight if you buy everything up front.** Etsy setup ($15-29) + Chrome Web Store ($5) + Shopify Partner revenue-share registration ($19) + a domain (~$10) comes to more than $30. The sequencing in Section 4 starts with $0 models and pays for later fees out of early revenue.

- **Faceless AI YouTube.** Since July 15, 2025, YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule demonetizes mass-produced, repetitive channels, including AI-voice slideshow formats. - **Google Play.** $25 fits, but new personal accounts must run a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days before production. ([Google](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en)) Possible later.
- **Amazon Associates as a primary model.** 1-4% commissions in most categories, plus three qualifying sales in 180 days to keep the account. ([Shopify guide](https://www.shopify.com/blog/amazon-affiliate-marketing))

## 2. Detailed profiles

### 1. AI-assisted freelance services (web apps, automations, landing pages, scrapers, data cleanup)
- **How it makes money:** Fixed-price gigs or hourly contracts. Over time, productized offers such as "Shopify speed fix, $300" or "n8n/Zapier automation, $500".
- **Startup cost:** $0. Fiverr and Contra are free. Upwork gives new accounts some free Connects; extra Connects cost about $0.15 each.
- **Accounts:** Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, Stripe/PayPal, GitHub, Vercel/Netlify free tier.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI agents write the code, deliverables, proposals and portfolio demos.
  - The human does ID/video verification, client messages and calls, scoping, final QA, and owns accountability.
  - Be transparent that you use AI tooling. Never misrepresent who you are or your credentials.
  - Upwork and Fiverr forbid account sharing and identity misrepresentation. The human must be the person running the account.
- **Time to first $:** 1-4 weeks. Early gigs pay least, and reviews have to be built up.
- **Revenue:**
  - Fiverr reported "surging demand" for AI agents, workflow automation and vibe-coding services in 2025. Spend per buyer rose 13% to $342 even as active buyers fell 13.6% to 3.1M. ([Fiverr Q2 2025 letter](https://investors.fiverr.com/static-files/342e4729-6a76-467f-9a80-65f23d7d8deb), [Kavout FY2025](https://www.kavout.com/market-lens/what-does-fiverr-s-q4-and-full-year-2025-report-really-tell-us))
  - Typical new sellers make $0-$1k in their first months.
  - $100/day takes about 2-4 steady clients. Top freelancers and productized agencies reach $10k-$50k+/month, but that ceiling depends on time or subcontracting.
  - I found no public median-income data for these platforms.
- **Risks:** Race-to-the-bottom pricing, cold-start without reviews, scope creep, disputes and chargebacks, account suspension for off-platform payments.
- **Policy risk:** Low-medium, if you're transparent and never take payment off-platform.
- **Scores:** F9 / S6. **Fastest real dollars.** Also funds the other models and gives you market research for them.

### 2. Etsy digital templates (printable planners, budget spreadsheets, Canva/Notion templates, wedding/teacher printables)
- **How it makes money:** $3-$30 instant downloads.
- **Startup cost:** The one-time setup fee is usually $15 and can reach $29 depending on the account ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/understanding-etsys-new-15-setup-fee/), [LitCommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-an-etsy-shop/)). Then $0.20 per listing, a 6.5% transaction fee, and about 3% + $0.25 processing.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI does the designs (HTML/SVG to PDF, Sheets formulas, Canva layouts), mockups, SEO titles and tags, and listing copy.
  - The human does ID, bank and tax setup, review of every product, and customer messages.
- **Time to first $:** 2-8 weeks. Etsy rewards a catalog of 50-150 listings.
- **Revenue:**
  - Cody Berman's documented new shop made $185 in month 1 and $1,000/mo by day 116, with $6,161 in 4 months. ([Making Sense of Cents](https://www.makingsenseofcents.com/2025/03/how-i-made-6161-in-just-4-months-with-a-new-etsy-printables-shop.html))
  - Amma Rose Designs reported $93.5k in one year from digital planners. Planprintland reported about US$56.7k in 2022. ([Selfwage](https://www.selfwage.com/how-much-can-i-make-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/))
  - Typical: about 65% of sellers make under $100/yr, and the average time to the first $1,000 is about 165 days. ([Printify](https://printify.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/))
  - Etsy active sellers fell 10.9% and buyers fell 5% in Q3 2025. The marketplace is shrinking while AI supply grows.
- **Risks:** Heavy saturation in generic planners, copycats, algorithm swings, and the mandatory 12% Offsite Ads fee once a shop passes $10k/yr in sales.
- **Policy risk:** Medium.
  - Etsy's Creativity Standards allow AI-assisted designs from your own prompts under "Designed by". You **must disclose AI use in the listing**, and you may not sell bare prompts. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/etsy-new-seller-policy-2024-generative-ai))
  - Secondary sources report tighter rules in 2025-2026. ([selfemployed.com](https://www.selfemployed.com/news/etsy-seller-ai-disclosure-fees-2026/)) Re-read Etsy's policy before listing.
  - Never copy trademarked or copyrighted designs.
- **Scores:** F8 / S6. Survivorship bias is strong here. The shops that win pick tight niches (e.g., "ADHD home-daycare planner") and stay functional, not decorative.

### 3. Direct digital products (Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy / Polar): Notion systems, Google Sheets tools, code boilerplates, niche playbooks
- **How it makes money:** One-time sales of $9-$199, bundles, and occasionally subscriptions.
- **Startup cost:** $0.
- **Fees:**
  - Gumroad: 10% + $0.50 on direct sales, 30% on Discover. It has been merchant of record since 2025.
  - Lemon Squeezy: about 5% + $0.50, merchant of record, owned by Stripe.
  - Polar: about 4-5% + $0.40-0.50.
  - Sources: [checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees), [userjot](https://userjot.com/blog/stripe-polar-lemon-squeezy-gumroad-transaction-fees).
  - A merchant of record handles sales tax for you, which matters for a solo seller.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI builds the product, landing page, docs, demo video script and launch threads.
  - The human sets up the account and payouts, chooses positioning, and posts personally in communities (Reddit/X/LinkedIn), following each community's self-promotion rules.
- **Time to first $:** 2-8 weeks, and only if you have a distribution channel. These platforms send little organic traffic.
- **Revenue:**
  - Easlo (Notion templates): about $500k+ cumulative, reportedly $10-20k/month at peak. ([Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/blog/can-you-make-money-selling-notion-templates), [Goodreads repost](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/25863570-this-guy-has-earned-500k-selling-templates))
  - Thomas Frank: reportedly $2.5M+ from Notion templates, but he already had a 2M+ YouTube audience, which is why he's an outlier. ([Millionization](https://millionization.beehiiv.com/p/5-steps-thomas-frank-used-to-make-2-5m-with-one-app))
  - Typical: Gumroad median creator $70/yr (2020). One 2026 third-party estimate says fewer than 5% make over $1k/month. ([InsightRaider](https://insightraider.com/en/data/gumroad-statistics-2026))
- **Risks:** No traffic, Notion-template saturation, refund abuse.
- **Policy risk:** Low.
- **Scores:** F8 / S7. **Shares inventory with #2:** the same products can be listed on Etsy.

### 4. Freemium Chrome extension
- **How it makes money:** Free core features, $3-$15/month Pro via ExtensionPay or Stripe.
- **Startup cost:** $5 one-time Chrome Web Store developer fee. Backend on Cloudflare/Supabase free tiers.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI writes the whole extension, privacy policy, store listing and screenshots.
  - The human does developer account verification, any trader/contact disclosure the store requires, and support.
- **Time to first $:** 4-12 weeks. Store search is the main free distribution channel.
- **Revenue:**
  - Easy Folders (folders for ChatGPT/Claude) reported $3,700+ MRR and $42k total revenue six months after launch. ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/easy-folders/6-months-post-launch-my-chrome-extension-has-hit-3-700-in-mrr-and-42-000-in-total-revenue--O3qs28VAnAkcJw0j--M))
  - GMass reported about $130k/month (2019), and Closet Tools about $38-42k/month. ([ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com/articles/browser-extensions-make-money))
  - Counterexample: one extension with 20,000 users makes $60 MRR after 10 years. ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-built-a-chrome-extension-in-2-weeks-10-years-later-20-000-users-10-paying-customers-60-mrr-YaK5THoaKqXgTlgBjHgO)) Free-to-paid conversion is often 1-2%.
- **Risks:** Google policy and Manifest changes, users who won't pay, clones, and the target site breaking your extension (e.g., ChatGPT UI changes).
- **Policy risk:** Medium. Chrome Web Store enforces single-purpose, data-use disclosure and minimum-permission rules strictly. AI-written code is fine.
- **Scores:** F8 / S8.

### 5. Apify Store Actors (scrapers, data extractors, automation APIs)
- **How it makes money:** Rental or pay-per-result/pay-per-event pricing. Apify takes 20% of profit after platform costs, and payouts are monthly. ([Apify help](https://help.apify.com/en/articles/8684010-how-does-actor-monetization-work))
- **Startup cost:** $0.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI writes and maintains the Actors (Crawlee/Playwright) and the READMEs.
  - The human sets up the account and payouts, and makes the legal and ethical call on targets: public data only, respect site terms, no personal-data harvesting or login-wall bypassing.
- **Time to first $:** 3-8 weeks.
- **Revenue:**
  - Apify paid developers **$563k in September 2025 alone**, and more than $4M in total. ([Apify Challenge](https://apify.com/challenge))
  - In 2023 the top independent creator made more than $4k MRR, and "several" made more than $1k/month. ([Apify blog](https://blog.apify.com/programmer-passive-income-web-scraping/))
  - Apify's own Actors are the biggest earners, so the median creator likely earns little.
- **Risks:** Target sites change and break your scrapers, competition from Apify's own Actors, legal exposure from scraping.
- **Policy risk:** Medium, mostly legal and terms-of-service.
- **Scores:** F8 / S6. This marketplace also tells you what data people will pay for, which feeds micro-SaaS ideas.

### 6. Framer templates (+ 50% affiliate)
- **How it makes money:** Paid templates on Framer's marketplace. Framer says it takes 0% of template sales, and creators also earn 50% commission on referred plan upgrades for 12 months. ([Framer Creators](https://framer.com/creators))
- **Startup cost:** $0, though you need a free Framer account and an application to become a creator.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI produces designs, copy and code components.
  - The human assembles the template in Framer's visual editor, submits it, and fixes issues from review.
  - Framer is a GUI tool, so this is the least agent-automatable model on the list.
- **Time to first $:** 3-10 weeks, including marketplace review.
- **Revenue:**
  - Framer paid creators more than $6.5M in 2025. ([createwith](https://www.createwith.com/tool/framer/updates/framer-2025-recap-100-features-65m-paid))
  - One public creator went from $11.60/month (June 2025) to about $2.8k/month (March 2026). ([Framer community](https://www.framer.com/community/creator/@amar-kzr/))
- **Risks:** Strict review, design-quality bar, competition from established studios.
- **Policy risk:** Low.
- **Scores:** F7 / S6. Shares a design system with #2, #3 and #8.

### 7. Micro-SaaS web app (Stripe)
- **How it makes money:** Monthly or annual subscriptions, or credit packs.
- **Startup cost:** $0 on a free subdomain (vercel.app, pages.dev), or about $10 for a domain. Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI builds the full app, landing page, SEO pages and onboarding emails.
  - The human does Stripe KYC, pricing, customer conversations, and the go/no-go on ideas.
- **Time to first $:** 1-4 months.
- **Revenue:**
  - Pieter Levels' Photo AI hit about $150k/month in September 2025 (self-reported). ([DEV](https://dev.to/promptway/he-built-a-flight-simulator-in-three-hours-and-hit-1m-a-year-in-17-days-then-it-went-to-zero-1b1l), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/how-one-photo-ai-app-generates-132k-monthly-after-70-failed-startups/))
  - He also had **about 70 failed projects** first, which is the survivorship point in one person.
  - Marc Lou reported about $1.03M in 2025 revenue across many products, partly verified by TrustMRR/Stripe. ([yespress](https://yespress.io/marc-lou.md))
  - Typical: 67.8% of TrustMRR startups have under $1k lifetime revenue.
- **Risks:** Building something nobody wants, AI-built clones, support burden, Stripe risk holds.
- **Policy risk:** Low, with real billing, clear refund terms and no dark patterns.
- **Scores:** F7 / S9. Has the highest ceiling of the low-risk options, but each launch has low odds of working.

### 8. Niche directory (featured listings, sponsorships, job-board add-on)
- **How it makes money:** Vendors pay $49-$299/month for featured placement, plus sponsor slots.
- **Startup cost:** About $10 for a domain. Hosting on free static tiers. Listing data comes from public sources you curate.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI builds the site, collects and verifies listings, writes useful comparison pages, and drafts outreach.
  - The human sends sales outreach. Cold B2B email is legal under CAN-SPAM only with honest headers, a physical address and an opt-out.
- **Time to first $:** 2-6 months, because traffic has to come first.
- **Revenue:**
  - OpenAlternative: about $6.5k/month on about 70k monthly visitors.
  - SaaSHub: more than $10k MRR from about 100 featured listings at $99.
  - Both figures are self-reported and summarized secondhand. ([Dirstarter](https://dirstarter.com/blog/how-much-do-directory-websites-make))
  - Most directories never reach meaningful traffic.
- **Risks:** Google treats thin, auto-generated directories as scaled content abuse, so each listing page needs real value. Slow SEO.
- **Policy risk:** Medium (search).
- **Scores:** F7 / S7. **It doubles as a distribution channel** for #3, #4 and #7.

### 9. Niche B2B newsletter (beehiiv / Substack)
- **How it makes money:** Sponsorships, beehiiv Ad Network and Boosts, and paid tiers.
- **Startup cost:** $0.
  - beehiiv's free Launch plan allows up to 2,500 subscribers but **excludes the Ad Network, Boosts and paid subscriptions**. Those need Scale at about $43-49/month, so upgrade only once revenue covers it. ([TechRadar](https://techradar.com/pro/software-services/beehiiv-review))
  - Substack is free and takes 10%.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI does research, drafts and curation.
  - The human sets the editorial voice and does final edits, because readers churn on generic AI copy. The human also sells sponsorships.
- **Time to first $:** 3-9 months. Sponsors usually want more than 1-5k engaged subscribers.
- **Revenue:**
  - Solo creator Matt Navarra earned $18k from Boosts in 6 months, per beehiiv's own blog. ([beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/boost-your-newsletter-revenue-with-beehiiv-boosts))
  - Substack has 50+ writers earning more than $1M/yr. ([Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/substack-users?authuser=0))
  - Counterpoint: one reviewer earned $138 total from the beehiiv Ad Network. ([behindrankings](https://behindrankings.com/beehiiv-review/))
- **Risks:** Slow audience growth, deliverability.
- **Policy risk:** Low, if you avoid purchased lists and spam.
- **Scores:** F6 / S8. This is the audience asset the other models share.

### 10. Shopify App Store app
- **How it makes money:** Monthly merchant subscriptions billed through Shopify.
  - **0% revenue share on the first $1M lifetime**, then 15%. A 2.9% processing fee applies. ([Shopify docs](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share))
- **Startup cost:** $19 one-time Partner registration.
- **AI vs. human:**
  - AI builds the app (Remix/React Router template) and the listing.
  - The human does Partner account verification, app review back-and-forth, and merchant support.
  - Hosting is needed. Free tiers can work at first, but reliability matters for merchants.
- **Time to first $:** 2-5 months. App review can take weeks.
- **Revenue:** Merchants are businesses with budgets, and many small apps reportedly reach $1-20k MRR. I found no recent verified indie case in my searches, so treat this as plausible but unconfirmed.
- **Risks:** Review rejections, support load, competition in every category.
- **Policy risk:** Medium (app-review standards).
- **Scores:** F6 / S9.

### 11. WordPress plugin (freemium)
- **How it makes money:** Free plugin on wordpress.org, paid Pro via Freemius or Lemon Squeezy.
- **Startup cost:** $0.
- **AI vs. human:** AI writes the PHP and JS. The human handles wp.org review, which can take weeks, and the support forums.
- **Revenue:** Freemius case studies (vendor-published, self-reported) claim many sellers pass $5k/month. ([Freemius](https://freemius.com/blog/success-story-ssl-zen-sagar-patil/))
- **Risks:** Security liability, slow review.
- **Scores:** F6 / S7.

### 12. Print-on-demand (Merch by Amazon, TeePublic, Redbubble)
- **How it makes money:** About $2-6 royalty per shirt.
- **Startup cost:** $0. Merch by Amazon requires an application and starts you on a tier limit.
- **AI vs. human:** AI creates text and illustration designs. The human checks every design for trademark and copyright problems, which is the main way POD accounts get banned.
- **Revenue:**
  - A Tier-1000 Merch account sells at about $500/month net. ([Flippa](https://flippa.com/9820958-merch-by-amazon-account-averaging-500-usd-in-royalties-per-month))
  - Redbubble/TeePublic owner Articore reported falling revenue, blamed on "deteriorating organic traffic". ([yespress](https://yespress.io/articore-group))
- **Verdict:** Heavily saturated with AI designs, so the ceiling is low. **Scores:** F7 / S4.

### 13. KDP books (human-edited)
- **How it makes money:** Royalties of 35-70% on ebooks, about 60% minus print cost on paperbacks.
- **Startup cost:** $0.
- **Policy:** You must disclose AI-generated content, and daily title-creation limits apply. ([Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/))
- **Revenue:** Written Word Media's survey found about 44% of authors earn $100/month or less, and about 8% earn more than $10k/month. Those top earners are mostly prolific fiction writers with years of backlist. Low-content and AI books are flooded.
- **Scores:** F6 / S4.

### 14. Free utility/tool sites
- **How it makes money:** Ads, or a paid tier behind a free tool.
- **Revenue:** AdSense alone is weak, with reports of about $0.50 RPM and "a few bucks" a month. ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/is-anyone-making-money-from-ad-revenue-these-days-58NSKCBN4gZLhh24SVTb)) Value comes from upselling to a paid tier or direct sponsors, which overlaps with #7.
- **Scores:** F6 / S5.

### 15. Adobe Stock
- **How it makes money:** 33% royalty, including on labeled generative-AI assets.
- **Revenue:** Per-download earnings have been reported falling to about $0.33. ([Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/significant-drop-in-download-earnings-is-anyone-else-experiencing-this-330172)) It's passive, but $100/day would take an enormous portfolio.
- **Scores:** F7 / S2. Not recommended except as a by-product of the design work.

## 3. Recommended top 10 to run in parallel

The ten split into two shared production lines that feed one shared audience.

**A. Code studio** (one AI coding setup, shared Stripe/MoR accounts, one component library):
- #1 Freelance services
- #4 Chrome extension
- #5 Apify Actors
- #7 Micro-SaaS
- #10 Shopify app

Freelance and Apify work show which problems people pay for. Extensions and SaaS are those problems turned into products. A scraper Actor can become a SaaS backend. A Chrome extension can be a SaaS's distribution layer.

**B. Design studio** (one AI design system with HTML/SVG-to-PDF pipeline, mockup templates, brand kits):
- #2 Etsy templates
- #3 Direct digital products
- #6 Framer templates

One product can go to Etsy and Gumroad, and Framer templates reuse the same visual language.

**C. Audience layer:**
- #8 Niche directory
- #9 Newsletter

Pick **one shared niche** that links the studios, e.g. "AI tools and automations for small e-commerce or service businesses":
- The directory lists tools in the niche and sells featured spots.
- The newsletter covers the niche and promotes your own extension, SaaS, Shopify app and templates.
- Freelance clients come from the same niche.

**The top 10, in launch order:**

| Wave | When | Model | Why this slot |
|---|---|---|---|

**Budget path:**
- Wave 1 costs $15-29 (Etsy) plus about $1-6 of listing fees.
- Wave 2 adds $5 (Chrome).
- The domain ($10) and Shopify ($19) are paid from first earnings.
- Out-of-pocket stays at or near $30.

**Realistic expectations:**
- Months 1-2: freelance work is the most likely source of the first $100 days.
- Months 3-6: the Etsy/Gumroad catalog and Apify/extension income may add $10-50/day if a few products hit.
- Months 6-12: $100/day combined is a reasonable goal, not a guarantee.
- Thousands per day almost always comes from **one** breakout product, usually SaaS, an extension, or an app, backed by an owned audience. Run the portfolio to find that product, then put your effort into it.

**Operating rules:**
1. Disclose AI use wherever a platform requires it (Etsy, KDP, Adobe Stock).
2. Check every design for trademark and copyright problems.
3. Scrape public data only, and respect site terms.
4. Use CAN-SPAM-compliant outreach only.
5. Never use fake reviews, sockpuppets or multiple accounts per platform.
6. Track revenue per hour by stream every month, and kill the bottom two streams each quarter.
