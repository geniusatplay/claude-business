# Florida Eligibility Check: Kalshi, Polymarket US, Crypto Exchanges, eBay Reselling

Research date: 2026-10-21. Not legal advice. Prediction-market law is changing week to week, so re-check before you fund an account.

## Verdicts

| Platform / activity | Verdict for a Florida resident | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kalshi, non-sports contracts (econ, politics, weather) | **ALLOWED** | Federally regulated exchange (CFTC DCM since Nov 2020). Florida has taken no action against it. 18+, KYC required. |

| Polymarket US, non-sports contracts | **ALLOWED** | QCX LLC d/b/a Polymarket US, CFTC DCM designated July 9, 2025. No Florida block found. KYC required. The international polymarket.com is NOT allowed (it geoblocks US users). |

## 1. Kalshi

- **CFTC status:** KalshiEX LLC was designated a contract market on Nov 3, 2020 (CFTC order; press release Nov 4, 2020). The CFTC amended the designation order on Jan 17, 2025. Kalshi's help center says it is regulated by the CFTC as a DCM.
  - https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8302-20
  - https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823765-how-is-kalshi-regulated
- **Eligibility:** You must be 18 or older and pass identity verification. Restricted jurisdictions are listed in Section VI of the Kalshi Member Agreement. The help center names no US state, Florida included, as ineligible.
  - https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823778-signing-up-as-an-individual
- **Florida:** Covers (updated Oct 21, 2026) says Kalshi is available to Florida users 18+, and that "no Florida state action is blocking Kalshi." I found no Florida cease-and-desist, lawsuit, Florida Gaming Control Commission (FGCC) order, or Office of Financial Regulation (OFR) action against Kalshi.
  - https://www.covers.com/betting/prediction-sites/usa/florida-kalshi
- **Federal backstop:** On Aug 11, 2026 the CFTC used its emergency authority (CEA §8a(9)) to direct Kalshi to keep operating normally after New York sought a nationwide halt.
  - https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9281-26

## 2. Polymarket US

- **CFTC status:** QCX LLC (Boca Raton, FL) received a CFTC Order of Designation as a DCM dated July 9, 2025. Polymarket acquired QCX, which now does business as "Polymarket US." Polymarket's own Terms of Use say Polymarket US is operated by QCX LLC d/b/a Polymarket US, a CFTC-regulated DCM, and that the international platform is separate and not CFTC-regulated.
  - https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/filings/documents/2025/orgdcmqcexorderofd250709.pdf
  - https://polymarket.com/tos
- **Eligibility:** KYC is required and the app is available on iOS, Android, and web (beta). I could not retrieve Polymarket US's own state list (polymarket.us does not expose it publicly). Third-party lists (OddsShopper, Next.io, Oct 2026) name AZ, IL, MA, MD, MI, MT, NV, and OH as restricted, mostly for sports. Florida is not on any list I found.
  - https://www.oddsshopper.com/articles/prediction-markets/is-polymarket-legal-in-the-us
  - https://next.io/prediction-markets/polymarket/legal/
- **Florida:** As of Aug 2026, the FGCC had not issued a cease-and-desist to Polymarket or any CFTC DCM. Its 2023 letters went to daily fantasy operators (PrizePicks, Underdog, Betr), not to prediction markets.
  - https://www.swrnn.com/polymarket/florida (lower-quality source)
- **Action item:** The in-app eligibility screen at sign-up is the authoritative check. Confirm Florida and the market categories offered there.

## 3. Florida actions relevant to both platforms

- **Seminole Tribe v. DraftKings (Broward County Circuit Court, filed Oct 8, 2026):** The Tribe alleges DraftKings Predictions sports contracts and Pick6 violate the 2021 Gaming Compact and amount to illegal sports betting. The suit is aimed at sports only and names neither Kalshi nor Polymarket. It is the most likely template for a future suit against them.
  - https://www.legalsportsreport.com/281066/draftkings-sued-by-seminole-tribe-over-sports-predictions-in-florida/
  - https://www.wftv.com/news/local/seminole-tribe-sues-draftkings-over-alleged-illegal-sports-betting/YXXF3DT7WJFKBH7E5MVHCMXEWU/
- **Legislation:** HB 189, a broad gambling bill, did not pass in the 2026 session. I found no enacted Florida law on event contracts.
- **Governor DeSantis** has publicly questioned whether sports event contracts conflict with Seminole exclusivity (per Covers; no date given).
- **Sports vs non-sports:** Every Florida-specific pressure point (the Seminole compact, Chapter 849 sports-betting law, the FGCC) concerns sports. Nothing in Florida targets economics, weather, or politics contracts.

## 4. National risks that could reach Florida non-sports contracts

- **Washington state (King County court, Aug 13, 2026):** ordered Kalshi to shut off sports, elections, and politics contracts in WA. This shows some states do go after politics contracts.
- **New York AG suit (Jul 31, 2026):** asks for a TRO halting all Kalshi event contracts nationwide. The CFTC's emergency order is the counterweight.
- **Circuit split on preemption:** the 3rd Circuit (Apr 2026) ruled for Kalshi. The 9th Circuit (Aug 2026) and the 6th Circuit (Sept 25, 2026, Ohio/Tennessee) ruled against it. New Jersey has petitioned the Supreme Court. Florida sits in the 11th Circuit, which has not ruled.
- **CFTC rulemaking:** a proposed definition of "gaming" (June 12, 2026) and a proposed rule on event contracts as swaps (comment request Oct 9, 2026). These could narrow which contracts may be listed nationwide.
  - https://www.sportsbettingdime.com/news/industry/cftc-exercises-emergency-authority-to-keep-kalshi-running-in-new-york/
  - https://www.paulhastings.com/insights/crypto-policy-tracker/white-house-reportedly-plans-crypto-and-prediction-markets-gathering-sec-postpones-regulation-crypto-vote-sec-staff-issues-no-action-letter-on-tokenized-fund-custody-and-cftc-invokes-emergency

## 5. What would change the verdicts

- **Downgrade to NOT ALLOWED / UNCLEAR:**
  - an FGCC or Florida AG cease-and-desist
  - a Florida court injunction (for example, a Seminole suit naming Kalshi or Polymarket)
  - a new Florida statute in the 2027 session
  - an 11th Circuit or Supreme Court ruling against preemption
  - CFTC rules that bar certain contract categories
  - a nationwide injunction in the NY case
  - Florida appearing on either platform's own restricted list or in-app geofence
- **Most likely first change:** sports contracts geofenced in Florida. Non-sports contracts would likely survive.

## 6. Crypto exchanges (Florida)

- **Law:** Florida Statutes Chapter 560 (Money Services Businesses), administered by the OFR. CS/HB 273 (2022, effective Jan 1, 2023) defined "virtual currency." Custodial exchanges that transmit virtual currency need a Part II money transmitter license. A person selling their own crypto in a two-party trade does not.
  - https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2022/273/Analyses/h0273z1.IBS.PDF
  - https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2022/5/florida-gov-signs-bill-defines-virtual-currency-eases-licensing-restrictions-virtual-currency
- **Coinbase:** its licenses page lists Florida OFR Money Transmitter Part II, FT230000152.
  - https://www.coinbase.com/legal/licenses
- **Kraken:** its disclosures list a Florida OFR MSB license, FT230000256. Its regulation page shows NMLS ID MTR1843762. Kraken is unavailable in NY and ME, but available in FL.
  - https://www.kraken.com/legal/disclosures
  - https://support.kraken.com/articles/where-is-kraken-licensed-or-regulated
- **Verify:** check both licenses on NMLS Consumer Access or the OFR licensee search before funding.
- **Tax:** Florida has no personal income tax. Federal capital-gains rules apply only.
- **Caution for cross-exchange arbitrage:** buying and selling crypto for yourself needs no license. Moving money or crypto for other people for a fee could trigger Chapter 560 licensing. See the Florida case that held selling bitcoin is money transmission (State v. Espinoza, as discussed at natlawreview.com).

## 7. eBay reselling (Florida)

- **Sales tax rate:** 6% state rate plus a county discretionary surtax.
  - https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/sales_tax.aspx
- **Marketplace provider rule (effective July 1, 2021):** eBay must collect and remit Florida tax on sales made through its marketplace. A seller who sells only through eBay excludes those sales and does not collect tax on them. You must register as a dealer if you also make taxable Florida sales outside a marketplace, such as local in-person sales or your own website.
  - https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/tips/Documents/TIP_21A01-03.pdf
- **Resale certificate:** the Florida Annual Resale Certificate (Form DR-13) is issued only to registered sales-tax dealers and expires Dec 31 each year. It lets you buy inventory tax-free for resale. Retail-arbitrage buyers usually have to register with the Florida Department of Revenue to get one. Registration is free and online.
  - https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/eservices/Pages/registration.aspx
  - https://floridarevenue.com/Forms_library/current/brochure/gt800054.pdf
- **Federal reporting:** eBay issues Form 1099-K once federal reporting thresholds are met.
- **Local:** a local business tax receipt may be required by your city or county.
